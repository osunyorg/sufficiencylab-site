Sufficiency Agora Lead
Sufficiency Agora Coordinator
WSL is launching a network of Sufficiency Agoras as pioneering local
spaces for dialogue, experimentation, and collective action on sufficiency. The
first pilot initiatives will be held in France, helping to develop, test, and
refine a model that can be replicated across municipalities and regions. In the
coming months, WSL aims to expand the Sufficiency Agora network to Switzerland
and other European countries, including Germany, the Netherlands, and Spain,
fostering a growing transnational community of practice around sufficiency and
democratic innovation.

Conceived as both a place and a programme, the Agora will serve as a
welcoming community space where sufficiency can be explored, debated, and
experienced in practice. Through citizen dialogues, deliberative processes,
workshops, and public events, it will bring together residents, local
organisations, public authorities, and researchers to collectively explore
pathways towards more sustainable, equitable, and resilient ways of living.

WSL is seeking a Sufficiency Agora Coordinator(s)  to play a pivotal
role in launching and developing this initiative.    Mission:  Under the supervision of the Executive Director of the French Sufficiency Lab and in close collaboration
with WSL's Dialogues & Politics team, the Sufficiency Agora Coordinator will
lead the practical development and day-to-day implementation of the Agoras in pioneering
local authorities.

The successful candidate will transform the Agora from a concept into a
vibrant community space and programme by developing local partnerships,
designing and facilitating participatory activities, conducting a territorial
metabolism study, and documenting lessons learned to support replication across
the World Sufficiency Lab network.

This role offers a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of
research, democratic participation, territorial innovation, and ecological
transition, while making a visible and lasting contribution to one of WSL's
most ambitious initiatives.

WSL is launching a network of Sufficiency Agoras as pioneering local spaces for dialogue, experimentation, and collective action on sufficiency. The first pilot initiatives will be held in France, helping to develop, test, and refine a model that can be replicated across municipalities and regions. In the coming months, WSL aims to expand the Sufficiency Agora network to Switzerland and other European countries, including Germany, the Netherlands, and Spain, fostering a growing transnational community of practice around sufficiency and democratic innovation.

Conceived as both a place and a programme, the Agora will serve as a welcoming community space where sufficiency can be explored, debated, and experienced in practice. Through citizen dialogues, deliberative processes, workshops, and public events, it will bring together residents, local organisations, public authorities, and researchers to collectively explore pathways towards more sustainable, equitable, and resilient ways of living.

WSL is seeking a Sufficiency Agora Coordinator(s) to play a pivotal role in launching and developing this initiative.   

Mission: 

Under the supervision of the Executive Director of the French Sufficiency Lab and in close collaboration with WSL's Dialogues & Politics team, the Sufficiency Agora Coordinator will lead the practical development and day-to-day implementation of the Agoras in pioneering local authorities.

The successful candidate will transform the Agora from a concept into a vibrant community space and programme by developing local partnerships, designing and facilitating participatory activities, conducting a territorial metabolism study, and documenting lessons learned to support replication across the World Sufficiency Lab network.

This role offers a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of research, democratic participation, territorial innovation, and ecological transition, while making a visible and lasting contribution to one of WSL's most ambitious initiatives.      

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and Establish the Agora.
  • Identify, secure, and coordinate a suitable venue or recurring community space in partnership with the municipality and local organisations.
  • Co-design the physical and social environment of the Agora to ensure it is welcoming, inclusive, accessible, and representative of the diversity of the local authority.
  • Contribute to establishing governance principles, participation mechanisms, and operating processes for the initiative.

Design and Deliver the Programme

  • Co-design and implement a programme of citizen dialogues, deliberative sessions, workshops, exhibitions, and public events focused on sufficiency-related challenges, including energy, housing, mobility, food systems, and urban/economic development.
  • Translate WSL research into engaging and accessible formats that encourage participation and collective learning.
  • Facilitate activities directly or coordinate external facilitators and speakers.
  • Plan schedules, logistics, communications, materials, and participant engagement.

Community Engagement and Partnership Development

  • Map and engage key local stakeholders, including residents, community groups, educational institutions, public authorities, cultural organisations, and local businesses.
  • Build and sustain relationships that foster trust, participation, and long-term collaboration.
  • Develop outreach strategies that actively engage underrepresented groups and strengthen inclusivity.

Urban and Territorial Metabolism Study

  • Design and conduct an interdisciplinary study of the local authority's urban and territorial metabolism, analysing the flows and stocks of energy, materials, water, food, goods, mobility, waste, and associated financial resources.
  • Combine quantitative and qualitative methods, including data analysis, mapping, interviews, field observations, and participatory inputs.
  • Identify key actors, governance arrangements, structural barriers, opportunities for transformation, and local sufficiency levers.
  • Produce accessible outputs, including maps, visualisations, dashboards, and briefing reports.
  • Contribute findings to WSL's research agenda, participatory indicators, Sufficiency Gap Reports, and local policy discussions.

Documentation, Learning, and Communication

  • Document activities, participation levels, outcomes, and lessons learned.
  • Collect participant feedback and contribute to evaluating the impact of the Agora.
  • Develop a practical "Sufficiency Agora Toolkit" to support replication by National Sufficiency Labs and partner organisations.
  • Contribute to communicating the initiative through articles, social media content, photography, and event promotion in collaboration with WSL's communications team.

 Profile and Qualifications

  • Master's student or final year Bachelor's student eligible to undertake a convention de stage with a French higher education institution.
  • Background in social sciences, political science, urban studies, environmental studies, geography, community development, sustainability, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in sufficiency, ecological transition, social justice, and participatory democracy.
  • Excellent interpersonal, facilitation, and stakeholder-engagement skills.
  • Comfortable interacting with diverse audiences and facilitating collective discussions.
  • Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage multiple activities simultaneously.
  • Proactive, resourceful, and entrepreneurial mindset, with the ability to work independently and take initiative.
  • Fluency in French and good working proficiency in English.

The following would be considered strong assets:

  • Experience in community organising, participatory processes, citizen engagement, or local development.
  • Experience designing or facilitating workshops, public events, or deliberative processes.
  • Quantitative and qualitative research skills, particularly in territorial analysis, resource-flow analysis, GIS, mapping, interviews, or fieldwork.
  • Familiarity with local contexts.
  • Experience producing communication materials, visualisations, or reports.
  • Strong alignment with the mission, values, and vision of the World Sufficiency Lab.

Internship Conditions

  • Framework: French internship (stage) under a tripartite convention de stage between the intern, WSL, and the host educational institution.
  • Duration: Approximately six months.
  • Working time: Full time
  • Allowance: Statutory internship compensation (gratification de stage) and applicable benefits in accordance with French regulations and WSL policy.
  • Location: WSL's Paris office and the Agora site, with occasional remote work arrangements where appropriate.
  • Some evening and weekend availability may be required for community events, with corresponding time off in lieu.
  • All activities are conducted in accordance with WSL's Code of Conduct, Ethics Framework, and Safeguarding Policy.

Application Process

  • Applications are open until September 30, 2026
  • Please send your CV and a short cover letter to info@thesufficiencylab.org with the subject line: "Sufficiency Agora Lead - [Candidate Name]".
  • Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis. 
  • Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview. Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis.
  • The World Sufficiency Lab reserves the right to close the recruitment process before the stated deadline if a suitable candidate is identified.
